FAQ
How do I cancel a registration?
Go to Sessions → Your registrations and tap "Cancel". Please cancel at least 24h in advance.
When will I receive my payment?
Payments are processed within 2 weeks of the session date by the lab's accounting team.
Can I change my lab affiliation?
Yes — contact your current lab manager who can transfer your account to another lab.
