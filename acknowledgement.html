Acknowledgement & Citation

If you use EXPLAIN in your research — for recruiting participants, running sessions, or managing data — please cite the platform using the references below.

📖How to cite EXPLAIN
Bayle, G., Vallanzasca, I. and Villeval, M.-C. (2026). EXPLAIN: An experimental platform for large-scale investigations in behavioural and experimental economics. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/explain2026
Use this citation when EXPLAIN is central to your data collection methodology.
Bayle, G., Vallanzasca, I. and Villeval, M.-C. (2026). EXPLAIN: Experimental Platform for Large-Scale Investigations (Version 1.0) [Software]. GATE · CNRS. https://explain.cnrs.fr
Use this format when citing the software itself (e.g. in a data availability statement or methods section).
@article{bayle2026explain, title = {EXPLAIN: An experimental platform for large-scale investigations in behavioural and experimental economics}, author = {Bayle, Gabriel and Vallanzasca, Iane and Villeval, Marie-Claire}, year = {2026}, doi = {10.xxxx/explain2026}, url = {https://explain.cnrs.fr} }
Participants will be recruited and experiments managed using EXPLAIN (Bayle, Vallanzasca & Villeval, 2026), an open-access experimental platform developed at GATE · CNRS · Lyon and hosted at https://explain.cnrs.fr.
Ready-to-paste sentence for grant applications and project descriptions (ANR, ERC, etc.).

🤝Funding & institutional support
EXPLAIN received initial funding from the DGRI (Direction générale de la recherche et de l'innovation) of the French Ministry of Higher Education and Research. The platform is now supported by CNRS, which funds the unit providing infrastructure, hosting, and long-term maintenance. Throughout development and after deployment, GATE · CNRS · Lyon has provided continuous support through its administrative and technical staff.
